OCEANWAY COMPLETE WEBSITE
=========================

This package includes:
- Home page
- Product catalogue with filters and search
- Individual product detail pages
- Wholesale page
- About page
- Contact and WhatsApp enquiry form
- Chinese / English language switch
- Mobile responsive layout
- 12 initial OceanWay products
- SEO page titles and descriptions

IMPORTANT SETUP
1. Open: assets/script.js
2. Replace:
   whatsappNumber: "60123456789"
   with your real WhatsApp number in international format.
   Example Malaysia: 60123456789

3. Replace all occurrences of:
   YOUR_EMAIL@example.com
   with your real email address.

PRODUCT PHOTOS
Current images are clean placeholder illustrations.
To use your real photos:
- Put images inside assets/images/
- Name them exactly as the model code:
  OW1087.jpg
  OW1089.jpg
  OW1091.jpg
- Update the .svg extension to .jpg inside index.html, products.html or product.html logic.

HOW TO PREVIEW
